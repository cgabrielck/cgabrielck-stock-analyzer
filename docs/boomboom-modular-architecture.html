Dual-clock · H1–H3

Research / Scan clock

Desk lifespan: backend/api/scan_scheduler.py. Freshness: SIGNAL_MAX_AGE_HOURS / SCAN_STALE_AFTER_HOURS in backend/api/research_jobs.py.

Owns H1 Propose and the as_of on Intent v0. Stale research fail-closes new buys.

Execution / Worker clock

Worker: backend/trading/engine/worker.py + cadence backend/trading/engine/cadence.py.

Owns H3 Merge consumption and L2 submit. Exits / SELL may still run when the research clock is stale.

Do not collapse the two clocks. A worker tick is not proof that Scan is fresh.

IntentStore (L1) vs OrderStore (L2)

IntentStore · L1OrderStore · L2

L1 Intent v0 · forbid L2 fields

Required on Intent v0: schema_version=intent.v0, intent_id, symbol, side, stop, conviction_tier, expiry, as_of, source, status.

Forbidden on Intent v0: quantity / notional, limit_price, order_type, order_class, broker_order_id, idempotency_key, filled_*, slippage_pct, commission, take_profit as a broker leg, OrderStatus values (draft, risk_approved, submitted, …).
